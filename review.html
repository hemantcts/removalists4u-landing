How this answers the brief

The page now presents Removalists4u as what it is: a service that connects customers with registered, vetted removalist providers, arranges one quote and assigns a suitable team. Fleet ownership and named crew claims are gone. Calling leads on mobile and the quote form leads on desktop, so each device has its own conversion path. Every proof point now appears once, at the moment it helps a decision.

Layout, typography and spacing were redone with fewer colours, fewer cards and much more white space. The mobile page is about 9,700 pixels long, compared with the 21,907 you measured.

What changed, section by section

Your feedbackWhat we did
01 HeroNew headline options and supporting copy built on the correct positioning. Your Google rating and awards sit in one quiet row.
02 Mobile heroCalling is the primary action, with the number shown as text in the header and on the main button. The online quote is a clear secondary link. The sticky call bar hides over the form and never covers content.
03 Quote formTwo steps with visible progress and entries kept when going back. Step 1 takes name, phone and email. Step 2 takes move date and consent. These are the same fields as your live page, as instructed. Suburb, property and access questions can join Step 2 if you decide you want them.
04 PricingTruck cards replaced by a rate table led by home size. Discounted rates are shown against the previous price. Visitors no longer pick a vehicle, and the single-item offer is gone.
05 ImageryInvented branded trucks, branded uniforms, and the founder and crew stories are removed. Branded packaging appears as a concept, and the rest of the imagery is natural photography.
06 TrustOne "Why choose" section. The evidence is distributed: ratings and awards in the hero, checks beside the form, a customer quote beside the prices, and move numbers in Why Choose.
07 ServicesFocused on house, apartment and office moves, with packing and interstate as a single line. Man with a Van and single-item promotions are removed, along with the repeated card buttons.
08 Service areasA working suburb check gives a real answer from your live suburb list and carries the suburb into the quote. Regions collapse to save scrolling.
09 StructureDuplicate sections merged, moving day shortened, and one type and spacing system used throughout. Mobile has its own layouts rather than a shrunken desktop.
10 TechnicalHeading links clear the fixed header, and there are no empty links. Images are optimised and load as needed. Calls, partial enquiries and completed quotes are tracked as separate events, ready to connect to your analytics.

What we need from you

  • Partners: real names and approved logos. The band currently shows empty slots.
  • Photos: authentic images of real moves. Current photos are licensed stock placeholders.
  • Packaging: approved box artwork and real photos. The box image is our 3D concept. Please confirm "The smarter way to move" as the line, and which brand name it should carry.
  • Offer terms: conditions on the discounted rates, any minimum booking, and which home size suits each vehicle.
  • Claim wording: exact scope for insurance and ID and police checks, plus the recipient and years for each award.
  • Badges: official Google, ProductReview and Word of Mouth artwork.
  • Details: terms page, email address and social links.
  • Connections: CRM access for full and partial enquiries, and your call tracking provider.
  • Campaign: whether interstate moves should stay on this page.
