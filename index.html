FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? A real person answers the phone 7 days a week.

How much notice do I need to book?−

We recommend 3 to 5 days' notice, but next-day and even same-day moves are often possible in most Melbourne suburbs. Call us and we'll check crew availability on the spot.

Is the price really the price? Any extras?+

Yes. You pay the half-hour rate shown, for the half-hours used. No fuel levies, no stairs fees, no weekend surcharges. If a move runs longer than estimated, the extra time is charged at the same agreed rate.

Are your movers trained and verified?+

Every removalist in our network is ID-verified, background-checked and rated by customers after each job. Crews that fall below our standard are removed.

Which truck size do I need?+

As a guide: 3-tonne van for a studio or a few items, 4.5-tonne for 1–2 bedrooms, 6-tonne for 2–3, 8-tonne for 3–4, and 12-tonne for larger homes and offices. Tell us your bedrooms and we'll recommend the right one.

Do you offer packing and unpacking?+

Yes. Choose full or partial packing with quality materials, and we can unpack at the other end too. Just mention it in your quote request.

Do you work weekends and public holidays?+

We're open 7 days a week including weekends and most public holidays, at the same rates.

Do you do interstate moves?+

Yes, we coordinate moves across Australia with reliable scheduling, careful loading and real-time tracking throughout.

What if something gets damaged?+

Your crew wraps and secures every item, and your team leader does a walkthrough at both ends. If anything does go wrong, call us the same day and our support team will sort it out with you.
